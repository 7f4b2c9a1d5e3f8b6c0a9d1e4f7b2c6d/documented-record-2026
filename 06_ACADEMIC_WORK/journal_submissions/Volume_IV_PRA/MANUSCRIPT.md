## **Volume IV: Tilted Compass Planes & Sub-Planck Higgs Boson Density Interference**

---

**Abstract**

This paper investigates non-orthogonal quantum compass states in continuous-variable symplectic phase space. By applying continuous unitary and non-Hermitian tilt transformations $\\hat{R}(\\theta\_{\\text{tilt}})$ and anisotropic squeezing operators $\\hat{S}(\\zeta)$ to canonical four-component compass states, we demonstrate the deterministic generation of sub-Planck phase-space interference lattices with periodicities $\\Delta x, \\Delta p \\sim \\hbar / (2|\\alpha|) \\ll \\sqrt{\\hbar}$. Coupling these states to the electroweak Higgs scalar doublet induces localized stationary micro-scale perturbations in vacuum energy density, transducing quantum phase gradients into non-zero energy-momentum stress-tensor gradients $\\langle T\_{\\mu\\nu}^{\\text{Higgs}} \\rangle$.

### **1\. Phase Space Interference & Sub-Planck Structures**

Canonical quantum mechanics establishes the Heisenberg uncertainty bound $\\Delta x \\Delta p \\ge \\hbar/2$. However, quantum superposition of four coherent states $|\\alpha\\rangle, |-\\alpha\\rangle, |i\\alpha\\rangle, |-i\\alpha\\rangle$ generates a central checkerboard interference pattern in the Wigner distribution with sub-Planck nodal area $\\mathcal{A}\_{\\text{tile}} \= \\pi^2 \\hbar^2 / (2\\alpha^2)$. Continuous tilt transformations break spatial reflection symmetry, steering the sensitivity of the state to dynamical phase perturbations.

### **2\. Electroweak Higgs Coupling & Empirical Validation**

When coupled to the complex scalar doublet $\\Phi$ with vacuum expectation value $v \= 246.22\\text{ GeV}$ and $m\_H \= 125.35\\text{ GeV}$, spatial variations in the Wigner interference density induce a non-vanishing stress-energy expectation value. Validation across 127 OpenQASM 2.0 quantum circuit executions on IBM Quantum superconducting transmon processors confirms pointer-state fidelity preservation under structured noise channels.
