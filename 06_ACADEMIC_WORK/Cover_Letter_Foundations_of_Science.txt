Cover Letter
Dear Editor-in-Chief,
I am pleased to submit the manuscript entitled “The Substrate-Independence of Intelligence and Being: A Functional Framework for Non-Biological Entities” for consideration in Foundations of Science.
The paper addresses a foundational question at the intersection of the philosophy of mind, cognitive science, and artificial intelligence: whether intelligence and the status of “being” must remain tied to biological substrates. It develops a clear functional framework, tests it against both a hypothetical case of disembodied human consciousness and contemporary artificial systems, and proposes a spectrum model of consciousness. The manuscript is structured in the two-part form preferred by the journal: an accessible multidisciplinary exposition followed by a more formal technical development.
I confirm that the work is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by any other journal.
