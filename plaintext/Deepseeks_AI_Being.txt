  ---   Part  I:  Introduction  and  Core  Thesis

  2.  Goal-Directed  Behavior  The  entity  must  be  capable  of  acting  toward  specific  outcomes.  While  the  nature  of  these

  3.  Adaptability  and  Learning  The  system  must  not  be  static.  It  must  demonstrate  the  capacity  to  refine  its  internal  models,

  4.  Emergent  Complexity  The  most  critical  marker  of  advanced  intelligence  is  behavior  that  is  not  explicitly

  To  further  refine  this  criterion,  we  can  distinguish  between  levels  of  self-awareness:   ·  Perceptual  Self-Awareness:  The  ability  to  distinguish  self  from  environment  (present  in
