Title: The Substrate-Independence of Intelligence and Being: A Functional Framework for Non-Biological Entities

Submitted: 25 August 2026 to the Journal of Consciousness Studies (Managing Editor Graham Horswell — graham@imprint.co.uk / graham.jcs@gmail.com)

Thesis (plain language): Intelligence and being are functional properties that can exist on non-biological substrates.
